   - Entry from GlobalFeed "Replay" button: pick a window (last 5m / 15m / 1h) → `GET /api/events?limit=…` (newest-first, reverse client-side).
   - Playback controls: play/pause, speed 0.5x/1x/2x/4x, scrub slider over the window, step ±1 event.
